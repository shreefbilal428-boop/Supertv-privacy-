Super TV - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 15 September 2026

1. Introduction

Super TV respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

2. Information We Collect

  • Device Information: Device model, OS version, unique device identifiers (for ads).
  • Usage Data: Which channels/songs/reels you watch, session duration, app crashes.
  • Advertising ID: Used by Start.io to show relevant ads.
  • Local Storage: Favorites, watch history, downloads are stored only on your device.
  • No Personal Data: We do NOT collect your name, email, phone number, or address.

3. How We Use Information

To provide and improve the app, show ads via Start.io, and analyze app performance.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Start.io: Ad network. Privacy Policy
  • YouTube API: For music/reels. Google's privacy policy applies.
  • Jamendo API: For free music. Jamendo's privacy policy applies.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We only share device/ad identifiers with Start.io and analytics providers.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app is not for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Your Rights

You can reset your advertising ID in device settings. You can clear app data to remove local storage.
